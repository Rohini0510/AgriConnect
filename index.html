SIH 2026 Abstract

Problem Statement SIH26033

Multiple intermediaries in traditional mandi supply chains reduce farmers' net earnings to ~60% of consumer spend while inflating prices and creating post-harvest losses up to 18%.

Team Nexora Solution

AgriConnect connects farmer producer organizations (FPOs) directly with bulk buyers using demand aggregation, shared logistics pooling, transparent time-bound auctions, and AI route optimization.

Cummins College of Engg. for Women, Nagpur NAAC A+
Architectural Solution

Core Solution Pillars

An integrated end-to-end framework eliminating middleman friction

1. FPO Onboarding & Capacity Building

Verify FPOs, digitize member directories, and provide multilingual training on quality grading and digital trading.

2. Demand Aggregation

Pool weekly orders from small retailers and restaurants into city-wise bulk orders for scale and predictable demand.

3. Shared Logistics Pooling

Consolidate loads across FPOs onto shared trucks, split freight costs fairly, and show transparent cost breakdowns.

4. Transparent Price Discovery

Time-bound auctions plus live mandi data (eNAM/Agmarknet) and grade-based pricing rules for fair rates.

5. Unified Digital Workflow

Single platform linking onboarding, demand, trading, logistics, and instant 3-day payments with full visibility.
