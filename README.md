## 🚀 Overview

**AgriConnect** is an AI-driven digital marketplace and pooled logistics platform that transforms fragmented smallholder farmers into organized, digitally empowered Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs). 

By connecting FPOs directly with bulk buyers and retail groups, AgriConnect:
- 📈 **Raises farmer net earnings by 15-25%** (% of consumer spend realization).
- 🚚 **Reduces logistics costs by 25%** via shared truck load consolidation.
- ⚡ **Accelerates payment settlements** from 14 days to **3 days**.
- 🍃 **Reduces post-harvest produce losses** from 18% down to **10%**.
- 🛒 **Increases direct buyer procurement** from 15% to **60%**.

---

## ✨ Key Features & Solution Pillars

1. **FPO Onboarding & Capacity Building**: Verifies FPOs, digitizes member farmer directories, and provides multilingual training (English, Hindi, Marathi) on quality grading and digital trading.
2. **Demand Aggregation Engine**: Pools weekly orders from small retailers and restaurants into city-wide bulk orders to give buyers scale and FPOs predictable demand.
3. **Shared Logistics Pooling**: Consolidates produce loads across multiple FPOs onto shared trucks with transparent freight cost splitting and AI route optimization.
4. **Transparent Price Discovery**: Integrates live mandi price feeds (eNAM / Agmarknet) with time-bound auctions and grade-based pricing rules.
5. **Unified Digital Workflow**: Links onboarding, demand, trading, logistics, and instant 3-day digital payments with full visibility for farmers.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Architecture

- **Frontend**: Responsive Single-Page Application (HTML5, Tailwind CSS, Lucide Icons, Chart.js, Canvas-Confetti).
- **Mobile Prototype App UI**: React Native / Native-style interactive simulator with multilingual support (English, हिंदी, मराठी).
- **Backend Architecture**: Node.js / Laravel REST APIs with JWT role-based access control.
- **Database**: Relational DB (MySQL / PostgreSQL).
- **AI / Analytics**: Demand forecasting, price trend prediction, and route optimization.
- **Integrations**: Payment Gateways, SMS/WhatsApp Alerts, eNAM & Agmarknet Mandi Price Feeds.
